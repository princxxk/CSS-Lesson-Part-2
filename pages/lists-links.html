Lists & Pseudo-Classes

Custom list markers and state-based styling allow you to create interactive buttons, form feedback, and targeted list rules without extra IDs or classes.

Pseudo-classes begin with a single colon (:hover, :nth-child(), :checked, :disabled).

Next Lesson →

Lists & Link States

  • list-style: Customizes markers (square, circle, none) and position.
  • Link States: :link, :visited, :hover, :focus, :active.
  • Structural Selectors: :first-child, :last-child, and :nth-child(odd/even/3n).
  • Form States: :checked and :disabled.
